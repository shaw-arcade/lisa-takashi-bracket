How to pick your 70 together
  1. Solo pass first, 20 minutes each. Lisa picks as Lisa, Takashi picks as Takashi, on your own phones. Start from the proposed 70 if you like, then swap anything for a bench photo. No talking yet. That keeps one person from anchoring the other.
  2. Send your picks. Tap Copy my link and text it to the other person. Opening the link imports those picks on their device.
  3. Together, switch to Final. Tap Start Final from agreements. Anything you both chose is locked in. Only the disagreements need a conversation, and there are usually fewer than 20.
  4. Argue for a photo, not against one. For each disagreement the person who picked it says in one sentence what it adds that nothing else in the chapter does. If there is no answer, it goes.
  5. Check the chapter counts in the sticky bar. If one chapter is fat and another is empty, the album will feel lopsided even if every photo is great.
  6. Copy the export at the bottom into your 37 Frames favorites.
Rules of thumb: every chapter of the day appears. Every parent, grandparent and sibling appears at least once. No two photos of the same moment unless one is wide and one is close. Mix wide scene-setters, mid shots, and tight faces. If a photo only works because you remember the moment, it may not belong in the book. If a photo is a little imperfect but makes you both laugh, it does.
